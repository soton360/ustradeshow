# Privacy Policy for U.S. Trade Show

**Effective Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026  

This Privacy Policy applies to the **U.S. Trade Show** mobile application (the "App"), developed for the **30th U.S. Trade Show 2026**, organized by the **American Chamber of Commerce in Bangladesh (AmCham)** in partnership with and co-sponsored by the **U.S. Embassy Dhaka**.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you register for admission and attend the event using our application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you use the App to register as a visitor or attendee for the 30th U.S. Trade Show 2026, we may collect the following personal information provided directly by you:

* **Personal Identifiers:** Full Name.
* **Contact Information:** Email address and mobile phone number (optional, with international country code).
* **Professional Details:** Company / Organization name and Job Designation.
* **Event Preferences:** Visitor admission pass type (Single Day Pass or Entire 3-Day Pass), chosen visit date, and official seminars you select to attend.

#### Information We DO NOT Collect:
* We do **not** collect precise GPS or background location data.
* We do **not** access your camera, microphone, photo library, or device contacts.
* We do **not** collect payment or credit card details (admission to the 30th U.S. Trade Show is 100% free of charge).
* We do **not** collect advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID) or track you across third-party websites or apps.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected through the App is used strictly for legitimate event management and admission purposes:

1. **Digital Admission Ticket Issuance:** Generating your unique digital entry badge and secure gate check-in QR code (`TKT-XXXXXXXXX`).
2. **Ticket Delivery & Confirmation:** Sending your official admission pass and registration confirmation to your verified email address and sending entry SMS notifications (if a mobile phone number is provided).
3. **Venue Gate Check-in:** Scanning and validating your ticket at the event entrance at the InterContinental Dhaka.
4. **Seminar Capacity Management:** Managing seat allocation and attendee headcount for business seminars held in the Crystal Ballroom.
5. **Attendee Support:** Assisting you with customer support, ticket re-issuance, or event inquiries.

---

### 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing brokers. Your data is only shared with:

* **Event Organizers & Co-Sponsors:** The American Chamber of Commerce in Bangladesh (AmCham) and the U.S. Embassy Dhaka, strictly for official event administration and security verification.
* **Authorized Service Providers:** Trusted technical partners managing server hosting, transactional email delivery, and SMS gateway infrastructure, bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
* **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, subpoena, or governmental regulation to protect venue security and legal compliance.

---

### 4. Data Security & Storage

* All communications between the mobile application and backend servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard **HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS)**.
* Generated digital passes are saved locally on your device using sandboxed storage (`SharedPreferences`) so you can access your admission QR code offline at the event gate.
* Server-side data is maintained in secure, access-controlled data environments with industry-standard safeguards against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Your Rights (Data Deletion)

We retain personal information only for the duration necessary to fulfill the purposes of event registration, admission, attendee reporting, and post-event administrative audit.

#### Your Rights:
* **Access & Review:** You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
* **Correction:** You may request updates or corrections to your registration details.
* **Deletion:** You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data from our active event database. To request data deletion, contact us at **faysal@amchambd.org** or **amchambd@amchambd.com** with your registered email and ticket number. We will process your request within 30 days.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for business professionals, trade delegates, students, and general event attendees. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete that information from our servers.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our App features or relevant legal obligations. Any updates will be posted within the App and published on the official event website with an updated "Last Updated" date.
